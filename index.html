Welcome

Hello, I’m Shilpa Kastha, an Associate Professor in the Theoretical Physics Division at the Saha Institute of Nuclear Physics (SINP), Kolkata. I study gravitational waves and what they can tell us about black holes and gravity.

My research focuses on the dynamics of compact binaries and the gravitational waves they emit. I develop waveform models, study black-hole horizons and ringdown, and use gravitational-wave observations to test general relativity.

RESEARCH HIGHLIGHT · SEPTEMBER 2026

Learning the waveform, from inspiral to ringdown

Post-Newtonian physics meets Kolmogorov–Arnold networks.

In our recent work, we use a Kolmogorov–Arnold network (KAN) to learn amplitude and phase corrections to an analytic inspiral baseline from numerical-relativity waveforms. The learned corrections are then stored as explicit splines, so evaluating the waveform no longer requires the network.

Arghya Chattopadhyay & Shilpa Kastha · arXiv:2609.29115
